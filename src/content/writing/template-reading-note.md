Notes exist so that reading something and having a reaction to it does not
require an evening. Three or four paragraphs is a complete note. If it wants to
be longer than that, it is an essay and it should be one.

## What it says

One short paragraph. Enough that someone who has not read the source can follow
you, and no more.

## Where I think it is wrong, or where it lands

This is the part worth writing. Agreement is less interesting than the specific
place your own experience pushes back, or the specific thing it changed.

## What it means for what I run

Tie it to something concrete you operate. This is the line that separates a
note from a book report, and it is why these are worth publishing at all.
